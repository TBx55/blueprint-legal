Support

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Blueprint: Life System is a local-first iOS app. There is no account to create or log into. Your goals, habits, planner, and journal stay on your device.

Email us at blueprint.app.help@gmail.com. We read every note. Please include your iOS version and the app version from Profile if you are writing about a problem.

Subscriptions

Blueprint Pro is an auto-renewing in-app subscription sold by Apple: $39 per year with a 7-day free trial, or $6.99 per month with no trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID.

To restore a purchase, open Blueprint Pro in the app and choose Restore purchases. To change or cancel a subscription, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Website checkout is no longer offered. If a charge from the App Store looks wrong, start with your Apple ID purchase history, then write to us.

Your data

There is no Blueprint cloud account. You can export a backup from Profile and import it again on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes local data unless you have a backup.

Updating from 1.0

Version 2.0 is a rebuilt, local-first Life System under the same App Store listing. It does not automatically import website account data from earlier versions.

Age rating

Blueprint is rated 9+ on the App Store and is intended for a general audience. It is not a medical or therapeutic service.
