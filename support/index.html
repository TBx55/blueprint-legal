Blueprint: Habit Builder

Support

Last updated November 28, 2026.

Blueprint: Habit Builder turns your habits into a drawing: every day you keep your habits draws the next lines. There is no account; everything stays on your iPhone.

Email us at blueprint.app.help@gmail.com. We read every note. Please include your iOS version and the app version (Archive, at the bottom) if you are writing about a problem.

Common questions

  • When does a day draw? When you keep every habit scheduled that day, or, if your build uses a minimum, at least that many (for example 6 of 8). Days with nothing scheduled are rest days: they don’t draw and don’t break your run.
  • Grace days: you earn one for every 10 days you keep (up to 3). Use one from Today to save a missed day; it shows as gold dots and your run continues.
  • Count habits: tap the habit to type any amount (decimals are fine) and edit or undo entries.
  • Your photo: photos are converted to line art on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Widgets: long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Blueprint.

Subscriptions

Blueprint Pro is an auto-renewing in-app subscription sold by Apple: $39 per year with a 7-day free trial, or $6.99 per month with no trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID.

To restore a purchase, open Archive and choose Restore purchases (or use Restore on the Blueprint Pro screen). To change or cancel a subscription, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. If a charge looks wrong, start with your Apple ID purchase history, then write to us.

Blueprint 2.0 data

Your goals, journal and planner from Blueprint 2.0 are kept read-only under Archive → Blueprint 2.0 archive, where you can export them.

Age rating

Blueprint is rated 9+ on the App Store and is intended for a general audience. It is not a medical or therapeutic service.
