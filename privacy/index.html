Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026. Operator: Tyler Brown.

This policy describes how Blueprint 2.0 handles information. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It replaces the previous January 30, 2026 notice for the 2.0 app.

In short

Blueprint 2.0 is local-first. The app does not create an account and does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking. For App Store privacy labels, the intended answer is Data Not Collected: the app does not send your journal, goals, habits, photos, or display name to us.

What stays on your device

What you enter in Blueprint — including your display name, goals, habits, planner items, journal entries, optional vision-board photos, cycle settings, and progress — is stored on your device. Backups are created only when you export them. We do not host those backups. If you uninstall the app without a backup, that local data is deleted.

If you pin a photo to your vision board, Blueprint reads the photo you choose. Photos stay on the device. The app asks for photo library access only for that purpose.

What we may receive if you contact us

If you email blueprint.app.help@gmail.com, we receive whatever you include in that message (for example your email address and a description of a problem) so we can reply. We do not use a support contact as a marketing list.

Subscriptions and Apple

Blueprint Pro is an auto-renewing in-app subscription sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment, your Apple ID, and related billing. We do not receive your card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that purchase. The app checks your subscription status on the device through StoreKit so it can unlock Pro features. Restore Purchases uses your Apple ID on the device; it cannot restore a purchase made somewhere else.

No analytics or tracking

The 2.0 app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising, or a crash reporter, and it does not use the advertising identifier for tracking. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-app tracking.

Children

Blueprint’s App Store age rating is 9+. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app keeps data on the device, a child using a shared or parent-managed device stores that data locally on that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update at this URL and change the date above.
