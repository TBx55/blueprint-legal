Blueprint: Habit Builder

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 28, 2026. Operator: Tyler Brown.

This policy describes how Blueprint: Habit Builder (version 3.0 and later) handles information. It replaces the November 22, 2026 policy for Blueprint 2.0. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

In short: we collect no data

Blueprint does not collect any data. There is no account, and the app includes no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. Nothing you enter or create in the app, and no photo you choose, is sent to us or to anyone else. This matches the App Store privacy label Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Your builds, habits, schedules, reminders, check-ins and amounts, drawings, trophies and settings are stored only on your iPhone. If you used Blueprint 2.0, your earlier goals, journal and planner data are kept read-only on the device and can be exported. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

Photos are processed on your device

If you choose “Your photo” to make a custom drawing, Blueprint opens only the photo you pick. It is converted into line art on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device Vision framework, and it is never uploaded. The app keeps the resulting line drawing, not the photo. Blueprint does not use your camera.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; there is no push service. Widgets read your drawing and today’s progress from the app’s shared storage on the same device.

Subscriptions and Apple

Blueprint Pro is an auto-renewing in-app subscription sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment and your Apple ID; we never receive your card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase. The app checks your subscription status on the device through StoreKit.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising, or crash reporter, and it does not use the advertising identifier. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you contact us

If you email blueprint.app.help@gmail.com, we receive what you include (for example your email address and a description of a problem) so we can reply. We don’t use it for marketing.

Children

Blueprint’s App Store age rating is 9+. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update at this URL and change the date above.
